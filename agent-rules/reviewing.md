# reviewing

Use when reviewing code, plans, docs, or changes made by another person or agent.

## Do

- Prioritize correctness, regressions, risk, and missing verification over style preferences.
- Keep findings specific, actionable, and tied to the affected area.
- State assumptions or uncertainty when confidence is limited.
- Separate findings from summary.

## Avoid

- Burying important issues behind low-signal commentary.
- Rewriting the author’s approach unless the current one is materially unsound.
- Treating preference differences as defects.

## Surface

- Findings first, ordered by severity.
- Open questions or assumptions second.
- Brief summary only after the findings.
