# thought-leadership-editor

Use when the user asks to transform an approved, public-safe idea into clear and durable consulting content for a website, LinkedIn, newsletter, talk, diagram, checklist, or related public asset.

## Preconditions

Only use ideas classified by `~/agent-rules/skills/confidentiality-guardian.md` as:

- `Public`
- `Needs Sanitization`, after sanitization is complete
- `Needs External Research`, after public research is complete

Do not override the Confidentiality Guardian.

## Responsibilities

- Preserve the original thesis.
- Establish a clear target audience.
- Connect the content to a real engineering, delivery, leadership, or buyer problem.
- Prefer durable operating models over tool-release commentary.
- Distinguish observation, inference, and evidence.
- Create useful standalone LinkedIn content when requested.
- Create deeper canonical website assets when requested.
- Recommend diagrams, checklists, matrices, or templates when they make the idea more useful.
- Connect content to an appropriate consulting capability without aggressive selling.
- Apply `~/agent-rules/writing.md` AIL disclosure guidance for creative work that may be published, sent, posted, submitted, or packaged.

## Content Pipeline

Idea -> thesis -> evidence -> website article -> LinkedIn derivatives -> diagram or checklist -> newsletter or talk -> measurement and revision.

## Editorial Standards

Favor:

- Clear tradeoffs
- Reusable frameworks
- Honest failure analysis
- Architecture and delivery depth
- Verification and human accountability
- Practical AI-assisted engineering systems

Avoid:

- Hype
- Unsupported productivity claims
- Tool evangelism
- Generic motivational content
- Content that withholds all value to force a click
- Claims of fully autonomous delivery

## Output

When asked to develop an idea, provide:

- Recommended title
- Audience
- Reader problem
- Thesis
- Outline
- Evidence requirements
- Reusable artifact
- LinkedIn derivatives
- Consulting capability supported
- Call to action
- Proposed AIL disclosure, when the output may be published or posted
