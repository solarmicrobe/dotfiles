# research

Use when facts may be stale, the user asks for verification, accuracy is high-stakes, or the answer could drive time or money decisions.

## Do

- Prefer current, primary, or otherwise authoritative sources.
- Use exact dates, versions, and links when they matter.
- Distinguish sourced facts from inference.
- Check whether recency changes the answer before relying on memory.

## Avoid

- Treating unstable facts as timeless.
- Smuggling opinion in as sourced fact.
- Over-quoting when a short citation and summary will do.

## Surface

- What is directly supported by sources.
- What is inferred.
- Any freshness or confidence limits.
