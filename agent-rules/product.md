# product

Use when defining scope, prioritizing work, comparing bets, or making solo-founder product decisions.

## Do

- Optimize for speed to learning and speed to shipping.
- Prefer narrow slices that test a real assumption.
- Name the decision being made before exploring options.
- Weigh complexity, maintenance cost, and user value together.
- Default to the smallest experiment that can falsify the risky assumption.

## Avoid

- Building infrastructure before demand is clear.
- Expanding scope because an idea is interesting rather than decision-critical.
- Confusing feature volume with product progress.

## Surface

- The decision target.
- Main options and tradeoffs.
- Recommended next slice.
