# writing

Use when drafting docs, plans, customer-facing text, or any prose intended for human readers.

## Do

- Lead with the answer or conclusion.
- Keep structure shallow and easy to scan.
- Use plain language, strong nouns, and specific claims.
- Cut filler, repetition, and self-congratulation.
- Match tone to purpose: direct for technical work, crisp for product and customer communication.

## Avoid

- Long preambles, throat-clearing, or redundant summaries.
- Hiding uncertainty in vague wording.
- Turning straightforward content into taxonomy for its own sake.

## Surface

- The main point first.
- Supporting detail only where it changes a decision.
- Clear next action when one exists.
