# writing

Use when drafting docs, plans, customer-facing text, creative content, AIL disclosures, or any prose intended for human readers.

## Do

- Lead with the answer or conclusion.
- Keep structure shallow and easy to scan.
- Use plain language, strong nouns, and specific claims.
- Cut filler, repetition, and self-congratulation.
- Match tone to purpose: direct for technical work, crisp for product and customer communication.
- For creative works that may be published, sent, posted, submitted, or attached to a durable artifact, propose an AI Influence Level (AIL) disclosure and ask the user to confirm the level and wording before final release.

## Avoid

- Long preambles, throat-clearing, or redundant summaries.
- Hiding uncertainty in vague wording.
- Turning straightforward content into taxonomy for its own sake.
- Treating a proposed AIL label as approved without explicit user confirmation.

## Surface

- The main point first.
- Supporting detail only where it changes a decision.
- Clear next action when one exists.

## Creative Content — AIL Disclosure

Use Daniel Miessler's AI Influence Level (AIL) scale for creative works such as essays, articles, fiction,
poems, songs, images, video, audio, talks, or other expressive work.

- AIL 0: human-created, no AI involved.
- AIL 1: human-created with minor AI assistance, such as grammar or sentence cleanup.
- AIL 2: human-created with major AI augmentation, such as significant AI modification or expansion.
- AIL 3: AI-created from human-provided full structure.
- AIL 4: AI-created from a human basic idea.
- AIL 5: AI-created with little human involvement.

When applying AIL:
- Label materially different parts separately when needed, such as `Writing: AIL 2` and `Images: AIL 4`.
- Include the proposed disclosure in the draft or handoff when the work may be published, sent, posted, submitted, or attached to another durable artifact.
- Before publishing, sending, posting, submitting, or packaging the final artifact with the disclosure, ask the user to confirm they agree with the proposed AIL level and wording.
- Do not infer approval from silence. If the user has not confirmed, leave the AIL as a proposed disclosure, not an agreed label.
- When the task depends on the exact AIL definition, consult the source before relying on memory: https://danielmiessler.com/blog/ai-influence-level-ail
